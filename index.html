5-point safety check before confirming your emergency dispatch

1. Do you currently have fever, cold, cough, flu, sore throat, or any active infection?

2. Have you slept adequately (6+ hours) and eaten a meal in the last few hours?

3. Have you consumed alcohol or smoked heavily in the last 24 hours?

4. Have you taken antibiotics, aspirin, or other prescription medicines in the last 48 hours?

5. Have you donated blood within the last 12 weeks (if man) or 16 weeks (if woman)?

Why Components Matter: Critical Shelf-Lives

Platelets (RDP / Single Donor SDP) 5 DAYS ONLY

Stored at 20°C - 24°C with continuous gentle agitation. Due to the rapid 5-day expiration, hospitals face chronic shortages during dengue/chemotherapy spikes.

Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBC) 42 DAYS

Refrigerated at 2°C - 6°C. Crucial for severe trauma, post-partum hemorrhage, and surgical patients.

Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP) 1 YEAR

Frozen at -18°C or colder. Contains vital clotting factors for massive transfusions and liver disorders.
